Inflo is a research project and web-application I developed over the course of my master's program at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (2017-2020). It was designed to be a social media platform optimized for the sharing and discussion of news content. It was released in beta at the end of 2019 and can be found at ~~inflo.news/login~~ [deprecated]. More information about the project can be found at ~~infloproject.com~~ [deprecated] or in at [this link](https://repository.ust.hk/ir/Record/1783.1-107312), which is my 93-page masters thesis that describes in-depth all elements of the project; conceptual, technical, and with a hypothetical business model were it to become monetized. 

The manuscript on the keyphrase extraction mechanism can be found [here](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.03781).

A demo of the keyphrase extractor (static) can be found [here](https://nicksukie.github.io/inflo/cat-keyphrase-labeling-demo.html).

A demo of the platform Ui can be seen [here](https://nicksukie.github.io/inflo/mainfeed_v22-demo_v3.html).

A video overview of the platform can be found [here](https://youtu.be/f84mIVltoqo).
